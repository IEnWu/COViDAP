> About COViDAP

COViDAP is a web tool for determining a given molecule’s drug activity against SARS-CoV-2 and its conformity with known drug behavior based on five drug-likeness tests.

How to Use COViDAP:

  1. Enter the SMILES notation of the molecular candidate.
  2. Click the “Submit” button to generate the candidate’s report.
  3. Receive the molecule’s predicted activity against SARS-CoV-2 and its conformity to known drug-likeness criteria.
  4. A SARS-CoV-2 drug candidate must be predicted to be active and pass all five drug-likeness tests.
